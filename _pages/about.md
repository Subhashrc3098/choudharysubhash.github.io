I am a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, currently under the supervision of [Divyang G. Bhimani](https://sites.google.com/site/divyangbhimani/home-1).

My research interests lie broadly in harmonic analysis and dispersive partial differential equations, in particular Strichartz estimates, orthonormal Strichartz estimates, and their applications to Schrödinger and Hartree equations on tori and waveguide manifolds.

Previously, I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at Banaras Hindu University.
